## What small thing is making everyday life better?


A pair of brown plastic Birkenstock Bostons that were too small for my husband, so he gave them to me. They’re basically slippers that you can also wear outdoors. You can wear them with or without socks. They stay on even if you walk briskly. They don’t rub the top of my foot like my last pair of Crocs did.


Brown is not the colour I would have chosen but I might try painting a design on them.


## What am I reading?


See [Bookshelf](https://app.notion.com/p/3c76227653ba810386f3f0c7780ffd4a) but I’m in a bit of a reading slump. I feel like I might have read all the good sci-fi and fantasy. I hope not.

Once I’ve finished the [one I’m currently reading](https://jillmetcalfe.com/bookshelf/a-memory-called-empire/) I’m going to give Dombey & Son a go. I love Dickens but haven’t ready any for years so it will be interesting to see if I can still hack it.


## What am I learning?


Ultraspeaking


I’m working my way through the [Ultraspeaking](http://ultraspeaking.com/) courses – currently on Fundamentals 2. When I started out, I remember saying that my main problem was that, whenever I had to perform in front of an audience, 90% of my brain would fill up with “AAAAAAAAAAGH!!!” leaving me with only 10% to operate basic life support functions and give the presentation. I realised recently that that feeling has almost entirely disappeared. It’s like magic. ✨


Irish fiddle


When I’m old I want to be able to play tunes in a pub with other musicians. So I’m starting now.
